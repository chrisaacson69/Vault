## Takeaways
- **Most of the game is deterministic; the play is getting the AI to focus on someone else.** Its pick and gates are computable (`ai_war_aim`); its dice (the 10% skipped pass, the veto, the 1-in-100) multiply; nature (plague, typhoon) is too high-variance to insure: a +2% margin does nothing when the hit is 25-50%. The empirical tables already hold those events at their real rates (Chris).
- **An early bad event sinks a one-fief game; a grown empire absorbs it** through Sends and depth. The opening is where insurance pays; later it costs tempo.
- **A label can carry two jobs by accident.** The old COMING was both the attack gate and the army's savings plan; the cleaner label exposed the second.
