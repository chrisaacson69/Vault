# Session log: NA1, the hold, the double attack, and rice (2026-11-18 to 20)

Continues [the empire planner and MIRR](./2026-11-18-na1-empire-planner.md) (#62-#70), whose open thread was the late stall: 9+ fief empires that out-earn the board and still age out. The numbers are in **`na1-decompiler/docs/synthesis-frontier.md` #71-#86**; this is the map. The benchmark moved from 17 starts to the **12 live starts** (240 games, skill 2): five starts never won in 26 races (#80).

## What happened
- **#71-#74, the empire planner tuned.** An empty army's morale Give was refused by the cartridge and wasted turns at three sites; closing them was the first significant gain (57 → 76). Donors gated by MIRR, the strike season priced, hire-and-Move, and any SAFE fief funding the unsafe ones first: **92 of 340**.
- **#75, the stall audited:** the ready striker held its line and did not strike, blocked by the whole-empire gate, the prize left exposed, and its own Assign turn. Assigning ahead lost games: the split is also the fief's defence.
- **#76-#80, the double attack** (Chris: *result = unsafe might need a double attack*). Follow-up Moves that mend the prize lost at every gate. Past the header wall the problem is the win, not the hold. The battle lab measured two waves: a weak 1/99 wave feeds the defender, a commander-only first wave does not, and two waves cost more than one. The second waves timed out because **the defender bribed them with the first wave's lost gold**; the held-back commander bribing away the spare gold (Chris's dump) won 52 of 52. In the game, two waves are priced right but rare by geometry: the cap almost never binds in the stalled games.
- **#81, Uesugi's openings** (Chris read his 20 games): the fixed Echigo battle plan (#44) was never in the whole-game runs. Wired in: 7 → 11 of 20, year-1 invasions 8 → 1.
- **#82, rice** (Chris: *total econ is the better measure; we lose with the bigger economy, so we are not spending well*). 51 of 147 lost games out-earned every rival combined. **40 of 78 lost attacks went out with under 2 days of rice**, starved on day 1, and each army joined the defender whole. The last rival grew on our own men until it capped. Carrying 10 days of ration: **93 → 135 of 240** (z +5.7), games lost at 16 of 17 fiefs 13 → 1.
- **#83-#84, rice logistics and promotion.** Rice to the attack line and a maxed fief's rice feed cost hire turns, even men-first. The proven rules were promoted (`--preset best`, rice and the Echigo plan by default): 140.
- **#85-#86, revolts and the later invasions.** A capital revolts only under morale 45 (the roll cannot fail at 45+), apart from a 1-in-1,000 fluke at about 5% a game; a morale guard ends the rest. 8 of 21 later invasion deaths were the defender on 1 rice: the Summer gift checked the share line before the gift, but the AI's gate reads min(rice, men). Keeping just the rice the share line needs: **149 of 240**.
