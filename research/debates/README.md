# Online Debates
> Analysis of online "bloodsports" debates — extracting signal from spectacle.

## Purpose

Online debates (often labeled "bloodsports") are semi-structured, entertainment-driven exchanges between participants who range from informed amateurs to semi-experts. The format rewards interruptions, rhetoric, and crowd performance over rigor.

But they have value. The participants often touch on topics explored elsewhere in this vault — economics, philosophy, legal theory, civilizational patterns — even if only tangentially. The goal here is to:

1. **Summarize** what was actually said (not what the audience thought was said)
2. **Analyze** the claims against our existing frameworks
3. **Filter** signal from noise — flag where participants are onto something vs. performing
4. **Cross-link** to vault research where the topic intersects with work we've already done

## Approach

Focus on **ideas, not individuals**. Participants are listed for attribution but the analysis is about what was said, not who said it or how they said it. Personal attacks, rhetorical style, and character critiques are ignored — the goal is to extract anything useful regardless of how it was delivered.

Each debate gets its own file. Structure:

## Method pages
Not transcript reviews and not doctrinal spin-offs — reusable debate craft, extracted from many specimens at once:

- **[Worked Examples — The Impossibility Floor, With Arithmetic](./voting-paradox-worked-examples.md)** — an **evidence bank**, built for the planned [CGP Grey STV](./cgp-grey-stv-too-good.md) refutation: eight ballot profiles that make the social-choice pathologies concrete, every number computed by [`tools/voting-paradoxes.py`](../../tools/voting-paradoxes.py) and re-derived on every `--selftest` run rather than asserted. **Two attacks kept deliberately separate:** *impossibility* (no method satisfies every desideratum, so "the fair system" names a forbidden corner) and *underdetermination* (**"STV" is not one rule** — the implementation choices the cartoon skips *decide the winner*). **Headline:** identical ballots, four ordinary rule-sets (Droop/Hare × Gregory/whole-ballot), **three different three-member councils** — plus isolations proving the **quota alone** and the **surplus rule alone** each flip the result; where the surplus is drawn by lot, *a recount of the same ballots can seat a different person*. Second-best: **one electorate, six methods, five different winners**, with a Condorcet winner present so "there was no majority will" is unavailable. Also carries a worked Gibbard–Satterthwaite manipulation (6 voters bury their own favourite and win), non-monotonicity (raising the winner on 12 ballots defeats them), an IIA-violation-plus-Condorcet-failure in a single profile (the center-squeeze shape), a tie-break-free no-show paradox, and a majority-criterion failure under Borda that the selftest found *as a failed sanity check*. **The discipline the page enforces — don't fight looseness with looseness:** only the IIA case is literally **Arrow**; manipulation is **Gibbard–Satterthwaite**, participation is **Moulin**, and monotonicity/Condorcet/majority are *criteria*, not theorems. Deployment order is theory-**last**

- **[The Load-Bearing Word](./the-load-bearing-word.md)** — every resolution has one term carrying the argumentative weight, and it is rarely the topic noun (*"is masculinity in crisis?"* is about **crisis**; *"poverty is a sign of exploitation"* is about **sign**). Whoever pins that word sets the win condition for both sides, and a round that never pins it can only be won on delivery — which is the mechanism underneath every layer-1 death. Sorts load-bearing terms into **four families with a different test each**: *degree* words (*too, works, fair, crisis*) need a **failure condition**, supplied by operationalising against the function the thing exists to serve; *quantifiers* (*impossible, all, never*) set the burdens asymmetrically, so one counterexample kills a universal; *evidential* words (*sign, indicates*) require the sign to **vary with what it signals** and mark a **departure from a baseline**; *modal* words (*should, ought*) require asking whether the outcome is a live choice at all — if data centers get built regardless, *should* can only range over **where**, not whether. Part 2 unifies the two dirty moves as one operation in opposite directions: **inflation** (widen the term until nothing can refute it, and the resolution goes vacuous — *luck* → "anything that happens") and **severance** (narrow it until the opponent's evidence falls outside, conceding the substance to win on scope — *algorithm* severed from human moderation). Nine dated specimens, including Chris's own poverty debate as the positive case, where the concession on *"sign"* was won early and *"made the rest of it much easier."*

- [The Coconut Island and the Manufactured Option Space](./coconut-island-and-manufactured-option-space.md) — *not a transcript review*: a fully-developed, named rhetorical-pattern thesis (manufactured option space; the "learn to fish" / "build the log cabin" retorts) that a Vaush stream merely *sparked*. - [Technology → Culture → Politics](../philosophy/dynamics/technology-culture-politics.md) — the causal-ordering thesis the [LaCorte review](./lacorte-urban-rural-divide.md) sparked: material/technological conditions are the deepest layer, culture the transducer, politics the lagging downstream expression — argue at the level of values and you argue about the symptom. Lives in Structural Dynamics (Trunk 4); cites the LaCorte video, [unreplaced-event](../philosophy/dynamics/unreplaced-event.md), and [symmetry-breaking](../philosophy/dynamics/symmetry-breaking.md) as specimens.
- [The Supply Omission](../economics/the-supply-omission.md) — the portable thesis the [Keen–PBD review](./keen-pbd-postkeynesian-capitalism.md) sparked: "demand-driven" is half an argument (demand is unbounded, so supply binds); hiding the supply ceiling is what makes state money-creation look free — the standard anti-MMT scalpel generalized to **"delete the inconvenient half."** Lives in Economics; the Keen debate is its first dated specimen.
- [Constructed ≠ Arbitrary](../philosophy/constructed-not-arbitrary.md) — the portable thesis the [Word War masculinity review](./wordwar-masculinity-crisis-owlish-garcia.md) sparked: "it's just a social construct" conceals the premise *constructed ⇒ not load-bearing*, which is false because constructions exist to **solve real problems** and are therefore constrained at design time *and* run time. Yields the operational definition the debate never produced — **crisis := a system no longer functions for the purpose it was built** — and a third diagnostic (functional failure) beside Trunk 1's convergence and collapse. Generalizes [constitutive/elective](../philosophy/morality/constitutive-elective.md) past the normative; the debate is its first dated specimen.
- [AI as a Cognitive Tool](../ai-as-cognitive-tool.md) — the portable thesis the [AI round](./wordwar-ai-making-people-dumber-charsky-bunn.md) sparked: **outsource computation, never verification.** Verification carries the weight twice — it makes offloading safe *and* preserves the skill, because checking the work **is** the practice. Carries the **car reductio** (atrophy is a trade, not a verdict), **wheelchair-and-car** (assistive vs. amplifying — a floor raised and a ceiling raised), **productivity-not-intelligence** (trace the arrows: "smarter" claims route through nutrition, not the tool), and **fix the instrument, not the tool**, bounded by installed-base resistance.
- [Reading Outcome Statistics](../philosophy/epistemology/reading-outcome-statistics.md) — the four diagnostics the [feminism round](./wordwar-feminism-relationships-ruelas-anton.md) sparked, now a Trunk-2 page one level below the Weighting Problem: jurisdiction **and** date; specify the window; stated reason ≠ cause; scope the beneficiary. They stack, so four accurate numbers can support a false conclusion.
- [Generational Attribution](../philosophy/dynamics/generational-attribution.md) — sparked by the Word War round *"Are Boomers Responsible for Today's Economy?"*, which was **left unreviewed**; this is the analysis it prompted rather than a review of it. A cohort big enough to be pivotal correlates with everything, so the resolution is near-unfalsifiable without a **mechanism, a differential, and a counterfactual** — and the differential test is the killer, since if any cohort of that size produces the same result, the finding is about majoritarian dynamics under a demographic bulge, not about a generation's character. Carries the **incumbent-vs-prospective** asymmetry (the elderly-oppose-universal-healthcare paradox dissolves once you notice they already *hold* Medicare — position in the queue predicts stance better than need does) and the **inheritance-timing shift** (longevity delays transfer past the years capital would compound, and late-life care erodes estates — dispossession with no villain, and the one version of the grievance that survives the counterfactual test).
- [The Represented Unit](../philosophy/morality/legal-theory/represented-unit.md) — the doctrinal thesis the [Jake the Lawyer 17th-Amendment review](./jake-lawyer-17th-amendment.md) sparked: what a bicameral **upper chamber** is *for* (representing the pre-existing power centers whose consent formed the union — Lords/states/**Bundesrat**) and what breaks when you swap the represented unit. Carries the voided-consideration charge, manufactured malapportionment, exit+voice, *may*-vs-*must*, and federalism's two purposes (proximity/sorting + surface-area/capture-ROI). Lives in Legal Theory; this debate is its first dated specimen.
