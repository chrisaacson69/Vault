# Where Concepts Live — the materialist's positive account of universals
> A concept is a trained detector in one mind; the sameness between two minds' concepts lives in the world they were both trained on, not in a shared abstract object. Two independently trained networks agree in the dense centre of the data and disagree at the edges — and the disagreement is a **prediction of the account**, not a defect in it.

## Why this page exists

The Dyer/Molyneux rematch (Word War II, 2026-09) turned on one question asked three ways: *where does the number seven live, as opposed to seven coconuts?* — *"a pattern that has what in commonality?"* — *"if [the molecules] are not identical, then how do we know that there's some commonality?"* The vault already held the **leveler** ([the parity move](./the-parity-move.md): "God grounds universals" relocates the terminus) and the **position** ([emergence + convergence](../logic-and-math/emergence-and-convergence.md)). What it did not hold was the positive account a materialist could state on stage and find *consistent* — which is Chris's stated aim here, not scoring the round: *"I am just trying to give a case a materialist would find consistent."*

## The account, in four moves

**1. Deny the general.** There is no free-floating *chair*. There are chairs, and there is a grouping in each mind. A's grouping ≠ B's grouping; even A's *this-chair* ≠ A's *chair-in-general* (token ≠ type). Conceptualism, stated plainly. Molyneux said it — *"they exist as patterns within the brain"* — and then did not defend it.

**2. Locate the sameness upstream.** Dyer never demanded that two brains be identical; he demanded to know *what the commonality consists in*. The answer is not in the brains. It is in the **world both brains were trained on**, plus the shared selection pressure. Molyneux had this too and did not recognise it as the answer: *"the brain as a material organ in a perfectly stable and predictable material universe evolves to detect those patterns."* Two minds sense the same thing; they agree on *some* elements; they do not agree on all. Both halves are expected.

**3. Agreement is functional, not structural.** Communication needs sufficient overlap for the task, not identical internals. *"Is what I'm talking into a microphone? Yes. Boom, we're done."* — the right reply, made without the mechanism under it.

**4. The differences are fine.** The lowest common denominator across all minds' *chair* would be nearly empty; across all minds' *seven* it is thin too — one mind holds it as counting, another as pairing-off. This costs the materialist nothing. It is the theist's position that has to explain divergence as *error about a fixed object*; the materialist predicts it.

## The trained-network model — the vault's transistor analogy, upgraded

[Emergence and Convergence](../logic-and-math/emergence-and-convergence.md) uses transistors: `2 + 2 = 4` as voltage, arithmetic emerging from arrangement, substrate-independent. That analogy is **deterministic** — same program, same result — so it explains convergence and says nothing about divergence. The character-recognition network is the better instrument because it is **stochastic**:

| Fact about trained nets | What it models |
|---|---|
| The same blank architecture, trained on the same dataset at two different times, ends with **different weights** | two brains, same world, different developmental noise — structural identity is not even available in principle |
| Both nets still read the dense centre of the data the same way | agreement where it matters; "we both made it to Nashville" |
| At the **edge cases** — the ambiguous glyph — the two nets **disagree** on whether it is a 7 | the disagreement is located exactly where the account says it will be |
| A "7" is a direction in activation space; every token of a seven excites it to a different degree | type/token — the general lives as a detector, the particular as an activation |
| The net is matter that picks out a type across tokens | a direct counter-example to *"matter itself cannot be a universal… it would be self-refuting"* |

The screen analogy (LEDs easy to describe, the picture not reducible to any one LED, the same picture on two different screens) carries the supervenience point — fix the LED state and there is no picture-fact left over — but its two screens agree because they share a **signal**, which makes it the deterministic case again. Keep it for the levels-of-description point; use the network for the divergence point.

## Where it gets hard — the three tiers

Dyer chooses *seven* and *non-contradiction*, not *chair*, because the cost of "the LCD is thin" rises as you climb:

| Concept | Does divergence hurt? | The materialist's line |
|---|---|---|
| **chair** | no — nobody claims a chair-essence | fuzzy cluster (family resemblance); a stool is a boundary case with no fact of the matter |
| **seven** | some — *"is your 7 different from mine?"* sounds bad | different internals, same **operational check**: count, then pair off. Pairing (bijection) and succession (counting) are two procedures with the same extension — two minds can hold *seven* by different routes and agree on every output — **and both routes have been formalised, as the von Neumann and Zermelo ordinals ([What Is a Number?](../logic-and-math/what-is-a-number.md)), where they agree on every finite output and come apart at `ω`**. The **fact** that there were seven objects is mind-independent ([category 2](./relational-objectivity.md)); the **concept** seven is not. Realism about facts, conceptualism about concepts |
| **non-contradiction** | most — this is where "invariant" lives | the vault's logic pages already show logics are engineered and plural in production ([logics in production](../logic-and-math/logics-in-production.md), [the consistency defeater](../logic-and-math/the-consistency-defeater.md)) — paraconsistent reasoners tolerate contradiction locally. Different "pictures" exist at the logic level too; the agreement is in the centre (explosion is useless for prediction), the divergence at the edge |

Clouds are the same phenomenon with the detector firing on a non-instance: same input, two people, two animals. That is a concept behaving as a detector *in* the mind rather than a property read *off* the world — pareidolia is evidence for the account.

## What the account does not claim

- **Not "truth is subjective."** Seven objects on the table is a mind-independent fact. Keep the fact/concept line sharp or the relativism charge lands.
- **Not "perfect universal consistency."** Molyneux claimed it (*"93 billion light-years… perfect consistency"*) because he thought he needed it, and that is what let Dyer run Hume on him. The consistent materialist claims **less**: agreement sufficient for the task, plus a mechanism for why agreement is high where it matters.
- **Not a substitute for the parity move.** Dyer's Hume question — *justify the move from particulars to universals* — is unanswerable by anyone; that is the leveler's job (providence is an induction on God's past faithfulness or a brute posit — same circle or same terminus). This page is what you build on the level field. Molyneux tried to skip the draw and go straight to the positive account, which is why it read as circular.
- **The double edge.** Dyer can say a net's "7" is only *correct* relative to labels a mind supplied — so who labels reality? Reply: evolution is unsupervised; the label is survival; and for seven the correctness check is operational and anyone can run it.

## Convergence limits — a partial answer to a standing open thread

The [epistemology README](./README.md#open-threads) asks: *every culture developed rain dances too — when does convergence indicate truth vs. shared cognitive bias?* The network model separates the two sources of agreement:

- **Agreement from the data** — the dense centre of the shared world. This is the convergence that counts as evidence for the territory.
- **Agreement from the architecture** — the shared inductive bias / loss function. Nets with the same architecture agree on **adversarial examples** that are not in the data at all, and those examples *transfer* between independently trained nets. Rain dances are the adversarial examples of human cognition: convergence produced by shared machinery, not by the territory.

The discriminator is therefore *does the agreement survive a change of architecture?* — a different substrate reaching the same result (Shannon's Boolean-algebra/circuit bridge; calculus from Newton and Leibniz) is convergence from the data. Agreement only among systems that share a bias is not. Partial, because the test needs an actually-different architecture to run, and for most of human cognition we have only the one.

## Open Questions

- **Is there an edge-case region for non-contradiction in *untrained* reasoners**, or does the plurality of logics exist only after formalisation? If children and pre-literate cultures never diverge at the non-contradiction boundary, the third tier is weaker than the table claims.

Strong support for "concepts differ, behaviour agrees"; not yet sourced into the vault.~~ **RESOLVED 2026-09-17** → [What Is a Number?](../logic-and-math/what-is-a-number.md). Sourced, and it came back stronger than expected: the two constructions are not arbitrary encodings but the two folk intuitions of number — von Neumann is *how many* (pairing), Zermelo is *how far along* (succession) — which are **the same notion below infinity and two different objects above it** (`ω` and `ω+1`: same cardinality, different order type). The account's "dense centre / diverging edge" holds with the edge **named** and the divergence a **theorem**. Caveat recorded there: Benacerraf does not kill Platonism, it upgrades it from objects to structures, so the reply must be *in rebus*.
- Does this account, stated on stage, survive Dyer's next move — the relocation to *"and what grounds the world's regularity?"* — without collapsing back into the parity move? (Probably not, and probably it should not try to.)
