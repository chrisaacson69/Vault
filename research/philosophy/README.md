## Branches by trunk

### Trunk 1 — Normative Ontology
- [Morality](./morality/README.md) — what's right and why; constitutive/elective distinction; performative grounding
- [Legal Theory](./morality/legal-theory/README.md) — applied Trunk 1 to legal norms (nested under morality)
- [Human Constancy — How Much Stays the Same, and How Would We Know](./human-constancy.md) — **the question is a ratio, not a binary**, and both poles (strong blank slate, strong essentialism) are strawmen nobody defends. Chris’s claim: *we are more the same than we are different* — with drift conceded up front, which is what makes it defensible. Central instrument is a **checkable asymmetry**: across a 3,000–4,000 year gap the **ends**-content of ancient texts is transparent (Gilgamesh’s grief, Ecclesiastes, Marcus Aurelius getting out of bed, the Ea-nāṣir complaint tablet) while the **means**-content (physics, humoral medicine, cosmology) is opaque — and both crossed the same gap through the same translators and survival filter, so if ends drifted at the rate means do, both would be equally alien. Layered model separating physiology / means / affect / expression, with the warning that change in **expression** is routinely misread as change in **affect**. Specimens: Reimer–Money (bounded, plus the second lesson — it was cited as proof of the *opposite* for thirty years because nobody checked), the **Flynn effect as means-side** (with reverse-Flynn as evidence it is environmental), and pair-bonding presented **two-sided** (Buss’s cross-cultural invariance vs. Eagly–Wood’s equality-gradient, synthesised as a fixed end pursued through variable means). Downgrades the [ratchet](../economics/subsistence-vs-participation.md)’s assumption from *constancy* to *ends drift slower than means*. Keeps its own strongest counter live: **fertility collapse**
- [Constructed ≠ Arbitrary](./constructed-not-arbitrary.md) — the trunk generalized past the normative to constructions at large (masculinity, currency, marriage, borders, language). Constructions solve real problems ⇒ constrained at design *and* run time ⇒ non-arbitrary ⇒ evaluable ⇒ **crisis := no longer functions for the purpose it was built**. Adds *functional failure* as a third diagnostic beside convergence and collapse. Runs **forward** too: the spec it yields supplies the mechanism a casualty-list argument always lacks, and defeats the stability defense (a machine can be reliably broken). Names **the severance** (defining a construction as "distinct from" its substrate installs arbitrariness by fiat), its twin **conflation** (one word for both ⇒ neither side can state its position), and the **three-layer stack** the dismissal exists to keep you stuck in
