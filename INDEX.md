    - [Human Constancy — How Much Stays the Same, and How Would We Know](./research/philosophy/human-constancy.md) — ***(thesis)*** **the question is a ratio, not a binary**, and stating it that way is most of the work, because both poles — strong blank slate and strong essentialism — are strawmen nobody defends. Chris’s claim: ***“we are more the same than we are different”***, with the drift **conceded up front** (physiology has changed, diet changes outcomes, measured intelligence has moved, *“can I say I think and feel like a caveman? I might even say we wouldn’t”*), which is precisely what makes it a small and defensible target rather than a constancy claim. **The central instrument is a checkable asymmetry, not an intuition:** across a 3,000–4,000 year gap the **ends**-content of ancient texts is **transparent** — Gilgamesh on Enkidu’s death, Ecclesiastes, Marcus Aurelius talking himself out of bed, Sappho, the Egyptian *Satire of the Trades*, and the **complaint tablet to Ea-nāṣir** (~1750 BCE) which reads exactly like a one-star review — while the **means**-content (ancient physics, humoral medicine, cosmology) is **opaque**, readable only as a historian. Both corpora crossed the same gap, in the same languages, through the same translators, under the same survival filter, **so if ends drifted at the rate means drift, both would be equally alien.** They are not, and the test could have come out the other way. The projection/survivorship objection is answered *partially* (it applies equally to the technical corpus, which stayed opaque) and the real settling test is named and **not yet run**: check the **mundane corpus** — receipts, administrative records, legal complaints, graffiti — which is unselected for resonance. **Layered model** (physiology / means / affect / expression) sorted by drift rate, with the load-bearing warning that change in **expression** is routinely misread as change in **affect** — varying marriage forms are a fact about the *means* of pair-bonding, not evidence the drive is constructed — plus the honest concession that the layers are **not sealed** (*“I won’t even dismiss that what we eat changes things”*). **Specimens:** Reimer–Money, bounded properly (refutes *strong* blank-slatism, does not establish nativism; unusually strong for n=1 because of the identical-twin control) **and carrying a second, better lesson — it was cited in textbooks as proof of the *opposite* for ~30 years because nobody checked the primary case**; the **Flynn effect as means-side** (~3 pts/decade, Flynn’s own “scientific spectacles” reading, with **reverse-Flynn in Norway/Denmark/Finland found within families** proving it environmental — a genetic change cannot reverse in twenty years), which converges with Chris’s own prior position from the [AI-dumber round](./research/debates/wordwar-ai-making-people-dumber-charsky-bunn.md) (*“what increases IQ are mostly health and genetic improvements”*); and **pair-bonding**, where the page was **corrected by Chris after first publication** and the correction flipped the evidence: Eagly–Wood’s convergence result (differences shrinking as equality rises) is **not** the standing finding — **[Walter et al. 2020](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0956797620904154)** (*Psychological Science*, 45 countries, Buss among the authors) found gender equality predicted sex differences in **actual partner age but NOT in preferences**. That split is exactly the page’s own model — *the end did not move, the realized outcome did* — so the "fixed end, variable means" synthesis is what the largest study found rather than a compromise between camps. And the broader **gender-equality paradox** runs the *opposite* way from social role theory: sex differences in personality, values and occupational choice are **larger** in more egalitarian countries ([Falk & Hermle, *Science* 2018](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aas9899) on risk/altruism/trust/patience; Stoet & Geary on STEM) — a theory predicting convergence in a domain showing divergence does not merely under-explain, **it has the sign wrong**. **But the paradox is itself disputed and the page says so**: a 2025 PNAS **Simpson’s-paradox** critique argues the association dissolves under proper analysis, a 2020 PNAS paper explains it by stereotypes with no innateness premise, and the strongest non-nativist reading is that **affluence changes what is rational rather than revealing what is innate**. Net: the convergence prediction has not held, the evidence sits closer to a fixed end than the page originally allowed, and *"innateness proven"* is **not** supported. **What it does for the vault:** it **downgrades an overstated assumption** — [the ratchet](./research/economics/subsistence-vs-participation.md) never needed *“human nature is constant,”* only *ends drift far more slowly than means* — and it supplies **[Trunk 1](./research/philosophy/the-four-trunks.md)’s input parameter**, since constitutive realism entails norms from *the structure of the relata* and is only as determinate as the property list; indefinitely plastic relata would collapse the trunk into the constructivism it explicitly contrasts with, so Trunk 1 had a standing dependency it was running on unstated. Also bounds [Constructed ≠ Arbitrary](./research/philosophy/constructed-not-arbitrary.md) by naming what constructions are constrained *against* at run time. **Keeps its own strongest counter live and unresolved: fertility collapse** — reproduction is the paradigm fixed end and every developed society is choosing dramatically less of it, so either the end moved or an unchanged end is being defeated by its means-environment; the means-substitution reading is *available, not established*. Honest status: **well-supported for affect, poorly tested for motivation**
  - [Constructed ≠ Arbitrary](./research/philosophy/constructed-not-arbitrary.md) — Trunk 1 generalized past the normative: "it's just a social construct" conceals the premise *constructed ⇒ not load-bearing*. Constructions exist to solve real problems, so they're constrained at **design time** and **run time** — therefore non-arbitrary, therefore evaluable, therefore **crisis := a system no longer functions for the purpose it was built**. Adds *functional failure* as a third diagnostic beside convergence and collapse (the continuous, present-tense version of collapse). Carries the design-to-environment retort ("are you a penguin?") and the *variance-at-the-margin* rule against low-N cherry-picking. **Runs forward as well as defensively:** "what problem was this built to solve?" yields a **spec**, which supplies the mechanism a casualty-list argument is always missing — and kills the stability defense, since a construction can fail its purpose at a perfectly stable rate for decades. Names **the severance** (arbitrariness isn't argued, it's installed upstream by defining the construction as "distinct from" its substrate — the design-time constraint deleted by fiat) and its twin **conflation** (one word for substrate + construction ⇒ attack the construction and you sound like you deny the substrate; neither side can state its position, so the clock goes to definitions). Plus the **three-layer stack** — definitional / mechanism / empirical — where the dismissal is a layer-1 objection whose function is to keep the argument there; counter is to stipulate their definition and proceed anyway

    - [Absolutes and Differentials — Frame Selection as the Political Crux](./research/philosophy/epistemology/absolutes-and-differentials.md) — **hub.** Chris’s thesis that a great many political disagreements are not about facts or values but about **which frame the facts get stated in**, with each side selecting the frame that favors it: the **absolute** (what is the level? is it being fixed?) licenses a *task*; the **differential** (compared to whom? moving which way?) licenses a *blame assignment*. Both can be true of the same data — real income rose *and* the gap widened — so the argument never resolves and each side reads the other as arguing in bad faith. **Why differentials generate strife structurally:** a gap has a counterparty built in, so it manufactures an enemy regardless of the numbers — class warfare is a differential argument *by construction*. **Second axis** (Chris’s Gulf examples): *one legible criterion standing in for a multivariate reason* — Iraq’s **WMD** (criterion falsified ⇒ the whole multivariate case reads as fraud) and its live inverse, *“Iran cannot build a bomb” and yet the war continues* (criterion **satisfied** while policy continues ⇒ it was always a proxy — the more diagnostic case). **War as the limit case** (Chris: *"wars tend to give simple reasons for every complicated matter"*) — three failure shapes: **trigger ≠ cause** (the archduke released pressure already loaded; it selected the moment and pretext, not the outcome), the **breadth dodge** (a criterion wide enough is technically satisfiable while substantively false — *motte-and-bailey with the motte pre-registered*), and recurrence across independent cases (Lusitania). Worked case: **Pearl Harbor as the archduke of WW2** — the oil embargo/asset freeze put Japan on a *use-it-or-lose-it* clock, the US was already fighting by proxy (Lend-Lease to China, the covertly-sanctioned Flying Tigers), so the strike selected the **moment and pretext**, not the outcome — mainstream historiography, requiring **no** claim of American foreknowledge. (The "bait" inference weakens on its own terms: *"obsolete battleships"* is **hindsight** — in 1941 the battle line was still taken to be the capital force, so that reading runs the scoreboard backward into intent.) **The guard is load-bearing:** pattern does **not** establish intent — minds are unrecoverable, chaos mimics design (*"it might not even be intentional"*), and each suggestive item has an innocent explanation. So the surviving claim is a **subtraction** (*the stated reason underdetermines the decision*), never a substitution (*the real reason was X*); otherwise it collapses into unfalsifiable conspiracy. **The guard is a standard, not a veto:** *"allowed to happen"* splits into a **weak** version (policy deliberately shaped to make war likely — evidenced by the embargo chain, Plan Dog, the Atlantic quasi-war, and Stimson's 25 Nov 1941 *"maneuver them into…firing the first shot"* diary entry) which **survives**, and a **strong** version (specific withheld foreknowledge of the attack) which does **not** — the first stays multivariate and is independently evidenced, the second needs a document nobody has. Chris's reading lands on the weak one, which is *compatible with* the archduke framing rather than rival to it. **Chris's cost/durability ladder** for how the single criterion gets manufactured: **fabricate** (WMD — cheap, falsifiable, collapsed in ~2 years) vs. **provoke** (shape conditions until the adversary supplies it — expensive but **self-authenticating**, since Japan really did attack, so it has survived 80 years) vs. **argue the actual multivariate case** (honest, and the weakest competitor because it will not compress into a sentence). Hence WMD as the *"poor man's"* method — and the uncomfortable corollary that a public which accepts only one-sentence reasons has **selected for** the first two. Only the single-hidden-decision form of substitution *re-commits the original error* — swapping one simple cause for another simple cause, sign flipped, so **conspiracy framing is single-criterion compression wearing the costume of sophistication**, the disease presenting as the diagnosis. Stopping at subtraction is therefore required by the analysis itself, not merely prudent — the same vacuity blade as *if everything is luck, nothing is luck*, turned on our own tool. **Placement (Chris):** this is the **lossy culture → politics arrow** — [Hop 2](./research/philosophy/dynamics/technology-culture-politics.md) applied to *justifications* rather than doctrines (a multivariate grievance reaches politics *as absorbed*, i.e. as one legible criterion; the qualifier is the part that costs something to keep, and **nobody has to intend it**). Contemporary specimen: ***"hands up, don't shoot"*** — the DOJ's two March 2015 reports are this page in one case: the slogan **unsupported** at that incident, while the *same department* documented a pattern of unconstitutional, revenue-driven policing in Ferguson. The compression was **costly to the cause** — it made the documented findings hostage to a checkable claim. **Politically symmetric** with WMD (same move, opposite coalitions). Open: Chris's *"lying about it no longer matters"* — if falsification has stopped costing anything, Tier 1 becomes cheap **and** durable and the ladder collapses; recency illusion is the null hypothesis and is not yet ruled out.     - [Reading Outcome Statistics](./research/philosophy/epistemology/reading-outcome-statistics.md) — four ways a **true number** yields a false conclusion, one level below the Weighting Problem (that page covers *aggregation*; this one covers reading a single statistic). (1) **A federated legal question is underspecified without jurisdiction *and* date** — fifty-one jurisdictions changing at different times means "is X legal?" has no truth value. (2) **Specify the window** — a rate measured across a rule change reports the *transition*; any stock released into a flow makes a transient that looks like a trend. (3) **Stated reason ≠ cause** — a survey category is a bucket, not a mechanism, and often contains the opposing argument. (4) **Scope the beneficiary** — individual experience and institutional health move independently, and reforms that change *who enters* change the denominator. manipulation is **Gibbard–Satterthwaite**, participation is **Moulin**, and monotonicity / Condorcet / majority are *criteria*, not theorems — bundling them as “Arrow” is how a correct critique gets dismantled on camera. Deployment order is **theory last**: rules-decide-the-winner first, five-winners second, theorems only as the explanation for why these are permanent rather than fixable.   - **[The Load-Bearing Word](./research/debates/the-load-bearing-word.md)** — **debate method page**, extracted from nine specimens. Every resolution has one term carrying the argumentative weight and it is rarely the topic noun; whoever pins it sets the win condition for both sides, and a round that never pins it can only be won on delivery. **Four families, four different tests:** *degree* words need a **failure condition** (operationalise against the function — *crisis :=* a system no longer serving its purpose; *works :=* achieves its stated goal, not exceeds it); *quantifiers* set burdens asymmetrically (**one counterexample kills a universal**); *evidential* words must **vary with what they signal** and mark a departure from a baseline; *modal* words require asking whether the outcome is a live choice at all (if the thing happens regardless, *should* ranges over **where**, not whether). ## Method
- [Method — a library of good procedure](./method/README.md) — how to run hard, agent-driven projects without drifting; living playbooks of warning signs and moves (career is one downstream consumer of this, not the parent)
  - [The Anchor Method](./method/anchor-method.md) — drift is the enemy; the gate is the anti-drift anchor that *proves grounding*; install the strongest anchor your grounding allows and upgrade toward a hard gate as it crystallizes; the director owns recalibration via divergent-approach tournaments. Born from the NA1 decompiler.
  - [Specimen & Thesis — The Two-Altitude Ledger](./method/specimen-and-thesis.md) — keep the specimen (dated, source-anchored review/project) separate from the thesis (portable idea it feeds), connected by a two-way ledger; the downward half (thesis→specimens) rots because hubs predate their evidence; promote portable ideas, don't trap them, and don't let auto-memory be the release valve. Debates pass; Monopoly/MoO/MoM fail.
  - [Author Web, Derive Hierarchy](./method/author-web-derive-hierarchy.md) — the organizing law for generative work: exploration discovers structure you can't pre-file, so keep the substrate write-optimized (web) and *derive* the read-optimized hierarchy (indexes, ledgers, audit) with tooling; a tree severs lateral radiation, the web holds it; the difficulty of scope is the cost of compounding — pay it down by deriving, not suppressing. Specs the derive-the-ledger generator.
  - [The Rosetta Stone Method](./method/rosetta-stone-method.md) — learn *new hardware* by anchoring the unknown machine against a **portable program you already understand** running on it: find the interpreter, match the opcode table byte-for-byte, follow a syscall into the chip — the known semantics decodes the unknown silicon, inverting learn-hardware-first. The Anchor Method applied to *learning* (the crib is the anchor; a cross-platform sibling is a free oracle). Depends on the corpus's portability discipline (a portable VM, not hand-ported native); KOEI is the exemplar (proven 6502↔65C816), generalizes to SCUMM / Z-machine / AGI. Born from the KOEI NES↔SNES reversal.
  - [Watching the Solver Learn](./method/watching-the-solver-learn.md) — the **observation** end of the anti-drift problem: iterating a solver converges at the speed you can *see what it is currently doing*, because objective/reward bugs are **behavioural** — nothing crashes, no test fails, every metric climbs, and the agent is useless. Names the layer the vault's solver projects lack (a live view of the artifact acting, mid-run and non-interrupting + training telemetry: raw *and* smoothed objective, a confidence/convergence signal, a bounded cost metric, an event log) against the batch-aggregate harnesses [pygone](./projects/pygone/README.md) and [MOO1](./projects/moo1-opening-optimizer/README.md) already have — batch answers *did it improve*, never *what is it doing*. Also: **fixed-size perception** (observation size must not be a function of world population), action rate as a *planning-horizon* choice not a performance one, and **run the curriculum with a from-scratch control** or it's an assertion. One evaluation law: **a benchmark the agent can shrink is not measuring the difficulty on its label** (the "16 zombies" test decreases monotonically because nothing respawns). Worked from Pezzza's PPO gladiator in C++/SFML.
